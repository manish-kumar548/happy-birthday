# Priye Birthday Story

## Build

- Create eleven story scenes plus the final sunrise, with timed dialogue, smooth transitions, parallax environments, active particles, bike travel, waterfall motion, decorative reveal, animated fireworks, curtains, and an interactive candle-lighting cake.
